Who We Are

More Than a
Dance Team.

ADJEI is a dance team created as an outlet to share and promote African culture and entertainment through Afrobeat dance. We are the friend that brings the energy and makes sure everyone's included.

We provide a space for students to embrace Afro-Caribbean culture, build genuine friendships, and find joy in movement. You don't need to be the best dancer — just willing to show up and grow.

The term "Adjei" is a Ghanaian expression — and an acronym representing everything we stand for.

Core Values

  • Cultural Pride

    Embracing where we come from — traditions, rhythms, and stories passed through movement.

  • Inclusion

    A space where all can try their best and be seen, regardless of experience or background.

  • Community

    Building strong bonds that support each other on and off stage — a home away from home.

Our Story

2023

In 2023, a group of six students recognized the absence of a consistent African dance team on campus. Inspired by their experiences in Columbus — where African communities are celebrated — they sought to create a similar space for cultural expression through dance.

The group debuted as a last-minute act for an African Night program, receiving overwhelming positive feedback and growing interest. Following multiple performance requests, they formalized into an official student organization.

Now in Season Three, ADJEI continues its mission to celebrate Afro-Caribbean culture, build community, and inspire the next generation to keep moving.
