The feedback I received from classmates was positive. Everyone responded well to the desktop OS concept, the branding, and the overall visual identity of the site. This is what I really wanted to land. The most specific and actionable piece of feedback came from Katy, who said that while double-clicking folders fit the desktop aesthetic, switching to single-click would make the site more accessible and easier to use for all visitors. I agreed with this and made the change. Folder icons now open on a single click, and the on-screen hint text was updated to reflect that. This actually improved the experience without losing any of the desktop feel, since the dragging, window stacking, and sound effects still carry the OS aesthetic.
Something that I was looking to add myself was sound effects which i did, successfully! I add a fairy dust and bubble pop effect when users click on folders. I think it's a really cute touch.
