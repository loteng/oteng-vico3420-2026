Hi! I'm Leticia Oteng a visual designer from Columbus, Ohio.

I'm passionate about creating clear, engaging, and impactful designs. I aspire to apply myself fully in every project, emphasizing information through a creative yet structured approach.

As a problem solver, I thrive on finding ways to improve and elevate projects, always asking: How can I capture attention and ensure instant comprehension at just a glance? Whether through branding, layouts, or digital experiences, my goal is to craft designs that communicate effectively and leave an impression.
